What authors actually complain about

Synthesized from r/selfpublish, r/KDP, ALLi threads, and comparisons of Book Report, ScribeCount, PublishWide & Publisher Champ.

Subscription tax on success

Competitors charge $10–$249/mo and often raise the tier as you earn more. TrackMyRoyalties is free, with every feature included.

Income ≠ profit

Sales dashboards ignore cover art, editors, AMS, Meta, BookBub, and ISBNs. We track expenses + ads so you see real net.

Password re-login hell

Amazon has no public KDP API; scrapers break and re-auth constantly. We use CSV / report upload — no platform passwords stored. Ever.

Dashboard fatigue

~15 minutes a day across stores adds up to nearly 100 hours/year. One filterable view for all platforms or one at a time.

Privacy anxiety

Local-first by default. Data lives in your browser. Export anytime. No “give us your KDP login” model.

Tax-season chaos

Year summaries, CSV/JSON export, and clean totals for Schedule C / accountant handoff.
